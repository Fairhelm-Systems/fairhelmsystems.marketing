Fairhelm Systems · Insights cover images
=========================================

Submit each prompt below to the image generator. Save the result as a WebP at
the exact path given (1600 × 900 px, under 250 KB) and commit it; the site
picks a cover up automatically when the file exists, and falls back to a
line-art motif when it does not. Keep the house style identical across all
images so the index page reads as one series.

HOUSE STYLE (prepend to every prompt)
-------------------------------------
Minimal abstract editorial illustration for an enterprise technology company.
Deep navy background (#070b14) with very subtle darker-navy gradient. Thin,
precise line art in pale steel blue (#6aa6dc) and a single muted teal accent
(#54bcab). Isometric or flat geometry, generous negative space, no people, no
faces, no logos, no text, no letters, no numbers, no UI screenshots, no glow
or neon, no gradients on lines, no 3D render look. Matte, calm, architectural.
Composition weighted to the right third so the left side can hold a title.
Aspect ratio 16:9.
